secondary_model controls whether your pipeline uses the newer SDV/challenger model as a gate on top of the main D-Ratings model.

In your setup:

secondary_model:
  enabled: true

means:

D-Ratings is still the primary model
a bet first has to qualify under your normal markets.yaml rules
then the secondary model can block it only when model disagreement is unusually high
if the secondary model is unavailable, the pipeline falls back to the primary D-Ratings decision instead of blocking the bet

With:

enabled: false

the selector ignores the secondary-model gate and uses only the primary D-Ratings output plus your market filters.

That is why your backtest file has it disabled: you wanted to evaluate the raw relationship between prob_bands, odds_bands, ev_bands, and kelly_bands without the secondary model filtering some candidates first.
